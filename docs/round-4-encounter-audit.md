## 2. Hard Blocker Summary

- **Encounter 1: Razorgore** — Hard blocker: Mind Exhaustion debuff (60s) + 30 eggs + bots cannot use Orb of Domination.
- **Encounter 2: Twin Emperors** — Hard blocker: Proximity heal (60y radius, 3.6s interval) + dual physical/spell immunity.
- **Encounter 3: Four Horsemen** — Hard blocker: 4 simultaneous corners required + unattended boss punishment spells (`Condemnation`, `Unyielding Pain`) + stacking Mark damage.
- **Encounter 4: Flame Leviathan** — Hard blocker: `Gathering Speed` stacking pursue + turret overload requires 2 launched passengers.
- **Encounter 5: Valithria Dreamwalker** — Hard blocker: Healing-only victory condition in classless/solo non-healer setting.
- **Encounter 6: The Lich King** — Hard blocker: Valkyr carrying sole player off platform in solo mode.
