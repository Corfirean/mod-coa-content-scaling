title: Dungeon trash no longer has several health boosts stacked on top of each other
---
Normal dungeon mobs had a dungeon bonus, an elite bonus and the old server dungeon multiplier all applied together, about 6.75 times their authored health at a full party. They now have their authored health, scaled to the party size; bosses keep their own higher health. Elites and world bosses outside dungeons lose the same duplicate elite bonus.
